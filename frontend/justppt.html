View and present your slides with ease

How to Use This Presentation Viewer

  • Start Presentation: Click the "Start Presentation" button to view your slides in fullscreen mode.
  • Open in Google Slides: Click "Open in Google Slides" to edit the presentation (requires Google account access).
  • Navigation: Use arrow keys or click on the slides to navigate when in presentation mode.
  • Fullscreen Exit: Press ESC or click the close button to exit fullscreen mode.
